Buy, sell, or trade on your campus

Shared live with everyone. Posting requires a verified .edu account, so every listing is tied to a real student — not anonymous spam.

Meeting up: trade in a public campus spot — a library lobby, student union, or dining hall — and bring a friend if you can. Many campuses also have a safety escort service; check with campus police/security if you'd rather not meet alone. Syllabank isn't part of the trade itself and can't guarantee any listing — a verified email confirms someone's a real student at that school, not that the item or price is as described.

Terms of Service

The short version

  • You must be 18 or older to create an account or use this service. We ask you to confirm this at sign-up — it's a self-attestation, not ID verification, and accounts found to be underage will be removed.
  • Syllabank is a listing board, not a party to any trade — buyers and sellers deal directly with each other and are responsible for their own transactions.
  • We don't verify that a listing's price, condition, or description is accurate — only that the poster has a real .edu email.
  • No payments are processed on this site. Nothing here handles or stores payment information.
  • Don't post anything illegal, fraudulent, or that isn't yours to sell.
  • Accounts or posts that get reported and confirmed as spam, scams, or fake may be removed at the owner's discretion.
  • This service is offered as-is, with no guarantee it will always be available or error-free.
Privacy

What we collect & why

  • No passwords are stored — you sign in with a one-time link sent to your email.
  • Your .edu email, to verify you're a student and let you log back in — never shown to other visitors.
  • Whatever you type into a listing, video, or report — visible to other visitors as described on this page.
  • Basic error logs (what broke, when) to fix bugs — no payment or government ID data is ever collected, because we don't ask for any.
  • Anonymous visit data (how many people visit, how long they stay) via Google Analytics — not tied to your name or email, used only to see whether the site is being used.
  • We don't sell your email or listing data to anyone.
  • Want your account and data deleted? Contact the site owner — until a self-serve option exists, it's a manual request.
